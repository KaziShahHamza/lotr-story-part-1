# Episode 1: The Last Light of Home

**Status:** Complete  
**Word count:** 846 (story only)

## Summary

On the eve of the midsummer harvest, young mapmaker Mara Vale discovers an impossible ember beneath the old watchtower. When armed riders arrive before dawn, Mara must choose between keeping its secret and helping her village survive.

## Story

At dusk, Mara Vale climbed the cracked stairs of the northern watchtower with a flask of lamp oil and a sky full of warning stars.

The tower was not used for watching anymore. For twenty years, the village had kept it as a landmark, not a defense. Yet tonight every window along the western road glowed with the same uneasy amber. People had seen riders moving beyond the wheat fields, too distant to identify and too numerous to ignore.

Mara found the final stair hidden under a drift of owl feathers.

At the top, an iron brazier waited beneath the broken roof. It had not held fire since the tower was abandoned. Now, its coals pulsed beneath a thin veil of ash, bright as banked blood.

She lifted the brazier's lid.

An ember rolled free and struck the stone. It did not scatter. Instead, it rose, floating to the height of Mara's face.

She expected heat.

She felt memory.

The ember showed her a tower of black glass standing at the end of a frozen plain. Around it marched shapes carrying green banners. Snow fell upward. From the glass tower came a woman's voice, whispering a name that made Mara's knees weaken.

"Mara Vale."

The ember winked out.

A bell rang below.

Mara ran down the stairs so quickly that she lost a boot. The village square had filled with frightened families carrying sacks and children. Elder Tomren stood beside the empty well, shouting orders to close the storehouses. In the lantern light, Mara saw soot marks on his face.

"Riders," he said as soon as he saw her. "The eastern road."

"How many?"

"Enough."

Mara looked beyond him to her younger brother, Fen, who clutched the brass needle of Mara's incomplete map. "I kept it safe," he said.

She drew him close.

Then the brazier flame appeared on the watchtower roof.

It burned white, throwing its beam over the roofs and fields. Every flame in the village answered: the hearth fires, the lanterns, even the candle inside the abandoned mill. Windows blazed from end to end of Vale Hollow.

This was not a warning fire. Mara recognized the old code carved into the watchtower ledger. It was a beacon.

And it had lit itself.

The riders emerged from the eastern road one by one. They wore dull armor and carried hooked lances fitted with red pennants. Their leader rode a pale horse to the edge of the square. He removed his hood, revealing a face crossed with pale scars.

"We seek the ember carried by Mara Vale," he said.

No one spoke.

Fen went rigid in Mara's arms.

The captain dismounted and approached the well. Mara stepped between him and her brother.

"I am Mara," she said.

The captain smiled without warmth. "You should not have answered the fire."

Behind Mara, Tomren drew the village axe from beneath his cloak.

More riders came over the eastern ridge.

Mara looked at the villagers, at Fen, at the map needle trembling in his hand. Her choice seemed impossibly small beside the choices she had seen in the ember: run, hide, or stand.

She pulled her hood low and raised the map needle toward the captain.

"If you want the ember, follow it."

The needle turned sharply north.

The captain turned pale beneath his scars.

A horn sounded from the western road. It was answered by a second horn farther away.

Riders were approaching from two directions now.

Tomren grabbed Mara's shoulder. "North road? It's no road at all."

"It is in winter," Mara said. "And winter is coming early."

She took Fen's hand. The needle pulled north, toward the dead path beyond the barley fields. Behind them, the beacon flared brighter, exposing every hidden face in the square.

At the village edge, Mara glanced back.

Tomren had raised the village axe. The villagers stood at the storehouses with what tools they had. For once, no one waited for the tower to give an order.

Mara led Fen through the gate.

As they crossed the threshold, the old well began to ring like a bell.

## Continuity notes

- Mara Vale is a young mapmaker and resident of Vale Hollow.
- Fen Vale is Mara's younger brother and carries a brass needle that belonged to Mara's unfinished map.
- The northern watchtower's ancient beacon activates for the first time in twenty years.
- The ember reveals the image of a black-glass tower, a frozen plain, green banners, and an unnamed woman who knows Mara's name.
- A scarred rider captain arrives seeking Mara and the ember.
- Hostile riders approach from the east, while a second unidentified force approaches from the west.
- Mara and Fen leave Vale Hollow by the northern road after choosing to investigate the brass needle's guidance.
