# Project Rules

These rules define the completion standard for Part I and its episodes.

## Episode limits and format

- Each episode's **story prose must be 1,000 words or fewer**. The title, status, summary, and continuity notes do not count toward this limit.
- Include an approximate story word count in episode metadata, for example: `**Word count:** 842 (story only)`.
- Use the episode format in the root `AGENTS.md`: title, status, summary, story, and continuity notes.
- Give every episode a distinct title and a focused purpose in the Part I arc.
- An episode should have a meaningful beginning and ending: resolve a local beat or provide an intentional, clear cliffhanger.
- Keep summaries brief and spoiler-aware. The story itself should be understandable without relying on the roadmap.

## Story and prose

- Write original fantasy prose, characters, dialogue, scenes, and plot developments. This project may be inspired by broad high-fantasy themes, but must not reproduce passages, distinctive dialogue, or closely recreate scenes from Tolkien's books or their adaptations.
- Do not present existing canon characters, settings, or events as original creations. If the project uses recognizable protected-world material, keep it clearly framed as fan fiction and do not imply official affiliation or endorsement.
- Use consistent point of view and tense within an episode unless a deliberate, clearly signaled change serves the story.
- Favor clear, economical prose, purposeful dialogue, and concrete sensory details. Avoid padding and repetition, especially to meet a word count.
- Keep violence, peril, and other mature material appropriate to the intended audience; flag a significant content concern in the episode summary or notes.

## Continuity and structure

- Maintain consistency in names, descriptions, abilities, motivations, relationships, geography, chronology, and established world rules.
- Before introducing a major revelation or changing an established fact, check the roadmap and related episodes/notes. Record consequential new facts in continuity notes.
- Part I must have a coherent opening, escalation, and resolution. Its ending should resolve the main Part I conflict while leaving a purposeful consequence or question for a later part.
- Keep the roadmap's episode plan current. Treat working titles and planned beats as adjustable proposals until established by written story content.

## Completion checklist

An episode may be marked `Complete` only when:

- [ ] Story prose is no more than 1,000 words and the stated count is checked.
- [ ] It follows the required episode format and has a useful summary and continuity notes.
- [ ] Spelling, grammar, readability, point of view, and pacing have been reviewed.
- [ ] Continuity has been checked against relevant episodes, notes, and the roadmap.
- [ ] The episode advances or deliberately resolves a Part I story thread.
- [ ] Prose is original and does not copy or closely imitate source text.
- [ ] The reading order and roadmap status are up to date.
