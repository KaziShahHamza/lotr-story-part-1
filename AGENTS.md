# AGENTS.md

## Project purpose

This repository contains an original, episodic fantasy story inspired by the themes of fellowship, courage, sacrifice, and the long journey home. It is organized as Part I of a larger story project.

## Writing requirements

- Write original prose and original characters; do not copy text, dialogue, chapter structure, or distinctive passages from existing works.
- Treat this as a transformative fan-fiction project and avoid presenting copied canon material as new writing.
- Keep continuity consistent across episodes, characters, locations, timeline, and unresolved plot threads.
- Prefer vivid but economical descriptions, purposeful dialogue, and clear scene objectives.
- Each episode should have a title, a short summary, and a complete narrative arc or meaningful cliffhanger.
- Record important new facts in the continuity notes when they may affect later episodes.

## Repository structure

- `README.md` — project overview and how to navigate the story.
- `ROADMAP.md` — planned episodes, milestones, and future writing tasks.
- `episodes/` — published and draft episodes for Part I.
- `notes/` — optional character, world, timeline, and continuity notes.

## Episode format

Use this structure for episode files:

```markdown
# Episode N: Title

**Status:** Draft | In review | Complete

## Summary

A brief spoiler-aware summary.

## Story

The episode prose.

## Continuity notes

Important facts introduced or changed in this episode.
```

## Review checklist

Before marking an episode complete:

- Check spelling, grammar, pacing, and point of view.
- Confirm names, dates, geography, and plot details against the continuity notes.
- Check that the episode advances the Part I arc.
- Remove accidental similarities to source-text wording.
- Update `ROADMAP.md` and the episode status.
