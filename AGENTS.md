# Agent Instructions

Before working on this repository, read:

1. [`docs/AGENT_GUIDELINES.md`](docs/AGENT_GUIDELINES.md) for how agents should plan, edit, and review work.
2. [`docs/PROJECT_RULES.md`](docs/PROJECT_RULES.md) for story, episode, continuity, and quality requirements.
3. [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md) and relevant episode or continuity notes before changing the story.

Follow those documents together. If instructions conflict, follow the stricter project or episode requirement and flag the conflict rather than silently changing established decisions.

Keep changes focused. Do not mark an episode complete unless it meets the project rules and has been reviewed for word count, continuity, originality, and basic prose quality.
